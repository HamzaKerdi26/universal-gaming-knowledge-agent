# Elden Ring Lore

The world of Elden Ring, the Lands Between, was once held together by the
Elden Ring itself, a source of order maintained by Queen Marika the
Eternal. When the Ring was shattered in an event known as the Shattering,
its many runes were scattered among Marika's children and their
followers, who became known as demigods. Each demigod claims a Great Rune
drawn from a shard of the original Ring, and each has since carved out
their own fractured territory, often twisted by the corrupting influence
of the power they hold.

The player character, the Tarnished, was once exiled from the Lands
Between long before the story begins, stripped of the grace that once
guided them. The shattering of the Elden Ring causes that grace to
return, calling exiled Tarnished back to the Lands Between to compete for
the demigods' Great Runes and, ultimately, the right to become the new
Elden Lord.

As is typical of FromSoftware's storytelling, much of Elden Ring's lore
is delivered indirectly — through item descriptions, environmental
details, and short, often cryptic dialogue rather than direct exposition.
Piecing together the relationships between Marika, her children, and the
various factions across the map is left largely to the player, which has
made community lore analysis a major part of how the game's story is
understood and discussed.
