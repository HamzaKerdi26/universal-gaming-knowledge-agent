# Elden Ring

Elden Ring is an open-world action role-playing game developed by
FromSoftware. It was created with worldbuilding contributions from
fantasy author George R. R. Martin and released in early 2022.

Players take on the role of a Tarnished, a wanderer drawn back to the
Lands Between after the shattering of the Elden Ring, a powerful artifact
that once bound the realm together. The world is ruled by demigods, each
holding a shard of the Ring, and the player's overarching goal is to
defeat them and restore the Ring in order to become the new Elden Lord.

Unlike FromSoftware's earlier Souls games, which are largely linear or
built around a hub-and-spoke structure, Elden Ring presents a large
continuous open world. Players are free to explore in almost any order,
discovering dungeons, ruins, and hidden areas at their own pace instead of
following a fixed critical path.

The game blends the studio's traditional difficult, deliberate combat
with open-world elements such as a rideable spectral steed, a full
day-night cycle, and a wide variety of side content scattered across the
map. It was widely praised for combining that traditional Souls-style
challenge with the freedom of open-world exploration.
