# Elden Ring Combat

Combat in Elden Ring follows the deliberate, stamina-driven style
FromSoftware is known for. Every action — attacking, blocking, dodging,
and using items — consumes stamina, so managing that resource carefully
is central to surviving encounters.

Players can choose from a wide range of weapon types, each with its own
attack patterns and associated skill. Weapons can be infused with
different Ashes of War, letting players change both the elemental damage
type of a weapon and the special skill attached to it. This makes build
variety much wider than in earlier entries in the series, since almost
any weapon can be adapted to different playstyles.

Magic is split into two broad schools: sorceries, which draw on
intelligence and tend toward direct offensive spells, and incantations,
which draw on faith and lean toward buffs, healing, and area-based
effects. Both require the right catalyst — a staff for sorceries or a
seal for incantations — to cast.

A significant addition for this game is Spirit Ashes, summonable allies
that can be called in most areas outside of certain restricted "no
summons" zones. Spirit summons range from weak but numerous skeletons to
powerful named allies, and they can meaningfully change the difficulty of
tough fights by drawing enemy attention away from the player.

Bosses are generally more aggressive than in prior FromSoftware titles,
with faster attack chains and larger movesets, which pushed many players
toward more mobile builds and heavier use of dodging over blocking.
